Design guide

Every new page or article should follow these rules so the site stays recognisably Ankate.

Corporate visual system

Primary: Navy #031C44 · Accent: Gold #D89A02 · Background: White / #F7F4ED · Text: #10203A

Do not use: green, teal, generic SaaS gradients, neon colours, rounded template cards, or unrelated stock photography.

Photography: use high-resolution, professional workplace imagery with African/Kenyan relevance where possible. Prefer people collaborating, advising, hiring, training or making workplace decisions.

Headlines: confident editorial serif. Body copy: clean professional sans-serif. Gold is an accent, not a second brand colour.

Blog structure: category → title → date → cover image → introduction → 3–6 sections → practical takeaway → CTA → disclaimer where appropriate.

Case study structure: hero image → challenge → approach → outcome → evidence/facts → client-approved testimonial → CTA. Never invent metrics or confidential details.
